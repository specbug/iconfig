Reading

R Sans starts from DM Sans, Colophon Foundry’s geometric family. The build tapers the terminals of twelve lowercase letters, raises the x-height by 4.8% so the lowercase matches Google Sans Text at the same size, and tightens the sidebearings of round letters by 12%.

18.5 / 1.62 · weight 450
425 in dark mode · ss03

Version 3 adds what a page needs from one face: tabular and oldstyle figures, small caps, superiors and inferiors, and thin and hair spaces. Its superiors are drawn at half size from a heavier weight, by a rule fitted to DM Sans’s own superior 41.
